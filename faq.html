Frequently Asked Questions

NEED TO KNOW

How can we help?

Find answers to some of the common questions about ordering, delivery and special orders.

01

How does delivery work?

Select the products you want, submit your order, and we'll prepare it for delivery to your hostel.

02

How much is delivery?

The delivery fee is ₦2,500. A separate service fee of ₦250 is added to each order.

03

Can I order multiple items?

Yes. You can select as many available products as you need before submitting your order.

04

When can I place an order?

Orders can be placed between 6:00 AM and 7:00 PM.

05

How long does delivery take?

Delivery is generally expected within the same day. Timing may vary depending on your order and when it was placed.

06

What if I can't find what I need?

You can use our Special Order option to tell us what you're looking for. We'll check if we can source it for you.

07

How do I pay for my order?

After submitting your order, you'll see the payment details and the exact amount to pay. Keep your payment screenshot for confirmation.

08

How do I track my order?

After successfully submitting your order, you'll be given an option to contact us on WhatsApp for order tracking and communication.
